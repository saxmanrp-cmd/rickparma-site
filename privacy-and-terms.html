Privacy Policy — Text Updates

Last updated: July 2026

Rick Parma ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information collected through our SMS/MMS text update service.

Information We Collect

When you text JOIN to (855) 924-2976, we collect your phone number. If you provide your name at any point, we may store that as well.

How We Use Your Information

We use your phone number solely to send you text updates about upcoming shows, music releases, and related announcements. We do not use this information for any other purpose.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or any personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Your information is only used internally to operate this text update service.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier plan.

Opting Out

You can unsubscribe at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

We retain your phone number for as long as you remain subscribed to this service. If you unsubscribe, your number is marked inactive and will not receive further messages.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the contact form at rickparma.com.
