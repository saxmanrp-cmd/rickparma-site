About

Built around the room.

Rick Parma is a Las Vegas-based singer, saxophonist and entertainer with more than three decades of professional experience. His performances move easily through R&B, Motown, soul, funk, pop, Top 40 and neo-soul, with formats ranging from solo singer/sax with tracks to full band. Rick’s approach is simple: read the audience, shape the energy and make the show feel personal rather than locked to a fixed set list.
