Short Bio

Rick Parma is a Las Vegas-based singer, saxophonist and entertainer with more than three decades of professional experience. His performances range from a polished solo singer/sax format with tracks to duo and full band, with music spanning R&B, Motown, soul, funk, pop, Top 40 and neo-soul. A regular presence in Las Vegas casinos, lounges, corporate events and private functions, Rick is known for reading the room and shaping the show around the audience rather than relying on a fixed set list.

Full Bio

Rick Parma has built a career as a vocalist, saxophonist and entertainer across clubs, casinos, festivals, corporate events and private celebrations. Over more than 30 years, his work has included Las Vegas performances at properties including ARIA and Westgate, festival appearances including Newport Jazz Festival, Long Beach Jazz Festival, Taste of Soul in Los Angeles and the Life Luxe Jazz Festival in Cabo, as well as a late-1990s touring stint connected with trumpeter Tom Browne and the band Heatwave. Rick’s musical center is R&B, Motown, soul, funk, pop, Top 40 and neo-soul, and his format can scale from solo singer/sax with tracks through full band. His approach is built around the room: read the audience, adjust the energy and make the event feel personal.
